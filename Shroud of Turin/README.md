<h1>Shroud of Turin and the Resurrection</h1>
You can access my lecture on the Shroud of Turin I delivered many years ago: <a href="https://youtu.be/vGL3BMY1ZcM?si=xw4pnEVRY-DgXyWq">click here</a> <br>It is outdated but will still prove a helpful introduction to the topic. The talk needed to skip over many details about the reconstruction of the Shroud's history, which is controversial. The general outline of the reconstruction, or something similar, is most likely true since icons were created using the Shroud image shortly after it was rediscovered during the reconstruction of Edessa's city gate. In addition, the first frontal image of Zeus was created immediately after the Shroud facial image was displayed above the gate in the early 1st century.
