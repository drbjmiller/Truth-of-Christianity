<h1>Shroud of Turin and the Resurrection</h1>
You can access my lecture on the Shroud of Turin I delivered many years ago: https://youtu.be/vGL3BMY1ZcM?si=xw4pnEVRY-DgXyWq. It is outdated but will still prove a helpful introduction to the topic. 
The talk needed skip over many of the details about the reconstruction of the Shroud's history, which is controversial. The general outline of the reconstruction, some something similar, is most likely true since icons were created using the Shroud image shortly after it was rediscovered during the reconstruction of the city gate.
